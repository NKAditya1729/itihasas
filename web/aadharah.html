Text: Sanskrit Wikisource contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0. The text is shown as Wikisource has it; only typing conventions are normalised (Unicode NFC, daṇḍa spacing, an ASCII colon used for visarga). Where the source itself is half-split or has typing errors, the verse is flagged for later correction rather than silently changed.
